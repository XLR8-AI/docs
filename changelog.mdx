<Update label="September 11, 2026" tags={["New"]}>
  Sentiment **Detailed Analysis** now lets you filter **Pros** and **Cons** with a **Self** / **Competitor** toggle. Self (the default) shows phrases about your brand; Competitor shows phrases attributed to other brands, with brand labels. **Export to CSV** follows the same filter.
</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" tags={["Improved"]}>
  In **Settings → Tracked URLs**, you can paste a list of URLs (one per line or separated by commas) and add them to multiple visibility or sentiment experiments in one submit. An optional bucket applies to every URL, or XLR8.ai assigns one per URL when you leave it empty.
</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" tags={["New"]}>
  On Analytics **Themes**, you can now filter clustered phrases by aspect, modifier, and intensity for the current Pros or Cons view. Filters apply together at the phrase level, so theme lists and the cluster map only show matching phrases.
</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" tags={["New"]}>
  **Reporting** now combines Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and monthly first-touch survey data in one place. Connect Google, choose your properties, and upload a CSV per month to see search performance, site traffic, and how people say they found you.
</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" tags={["Improved"]}>
  Publishing to Webflow or WordPress now saves as a draft by default, so you can review the CMS item before it goes live. Turn on **Publish immediately** when you are ready, or use **Publish Draft** later — that action defaults to going live. Updating an existing Webflow item also keeps its current draft or live status instead of publishing it automatically.
</Update>

<Update label="September 9, 2026" tags={["Improved"]}>
  Organization members can now approve pending **AI Brand Learnings** and edit their text in Settings. Dismissing a learning, adding one manually, importing from a document, and deleting remain limited to admins.
</Update>

<Update label="September 9, 2026" tags={["Improved"]}>
  Query set rows on Query Generation are fully clickable — click anywhere on a row to open the set. Download CSV and archive appear when you hover a row, and those actions stay on the list.
</Update>

<Update label="September 8, 2026" tags={["New"]}>
  You can now generate and optimize **PDF** content — reports, whitepapers, and other document-style pieces — then download the finished work as a letter-sized PDF from the content editor, alongside Markdown and HTML.
</Update>

<Update label="September 7, 2026" tags={["New"]}>
  The theme cluster map on Analytics now opens in 3D so you can rotate clusters on larger sentiment experiments. Switch to 2D when you want to pan and zoom.

  When you view **Cons**, a **Cons per theme** chart shows how many unique criticisms fall in each cluster. Outliers stay hidden until you turn on **Outliers**.
</Update>
